# 行为准则

Code of Conduct

AgriMart（农惠达）希望为农资电商与农机共享的学习、实践和协作提供友善的交流环境。无论参与者的经验、身份、性别、年龄、地域、语言、身体状况或观点如何，都应受到尊重。本准则适用于项目的 Issue、Pull Request、代码评审、文档讨论，以及代表项目参与的交流活动。

AgriMart aims to provide a welcoming environment for learning, practice, and collaboration around agricultural e-commerce and shared machinery. Everyone deserves respect regardless of their experience, identity, gender, age, region, language, physical condition, or views. This code applies to project issues, pull requests, code reviews, documentation discussions, and activities where participants represent the project.

## 我们期待的行为

Expected Behavior

- 使用友善、清晰的语言，尊重新手、不同技术背景的参与者和农业场景中的实际使用者。

  Use friendly, clear language. Respect newcomers, participants with different technical backgrounds, and the people who use the software in agricultural settings.

- 围绕代码、事实和可复现的结果讨论问题；提出批评时说明原因，尽可能给出改进建议。

  Keep discussions focused on code, facts, and reproducible results. Explain the reasoning behind criticism and suggest improvements where possible.

- 接受建设性反馈，及时纠正错误，尊重他人的时间、署名和劳动成果。

  Accept constructive feedback, correct mistakes promptly, and respect other people's time, attribution, and work.

- 区分已实现功能、开发计划与开发起步阶段设想；如实说明测试结果和系统限制。

  Distinguish implemented features from development plans and initial development proposals. Report test results and system limitations accurately.

- 使用虚构或脱敏的账号、订单与收货数据，保护农户、商家、农机拥有者及其他参与者的隐私。

  Use fictional or redacted account, order, and delivery data to protect the privacy of farmers, merchants, machinery owners, and other participants.

- 尊重原创和第三方许可；学习、引用和改编时标注来源，遵守项目开发起步阶段的诚信与署名要求。

  Respect original work and third-party licenses. Credit sources when learning from, quoting, or adapting material, and follow the project's integrity and attribution requirements during initial development.

## 不可接受的行为

Unacceptable Behavior

- 人身攻击、歧视、羞辱、威胁、持续骚扰，以及带有性暗示或性骚扰性质的言行。

  Personal attacks, discrimination, humiliation, threats, persistent harassment, and sexually suggestive or harassing conduct.

- 发布他人的姓名、手机号、住址、账号、私密通信或其他个人信息，而未获得明确同意。

  Publishing another person's name, phone number, address, account details, private communications, or other personal information without explicit consent.

- 因技术分歧而恶意纠缠、报复举报者、组织围攻，或持续干扰正常协作。

  Persistently targeting someone over a technical disagreement, retaliating against reporters, organizing group harassment, or repeatedly disrupting collaboration.

- 抄袭、冒用他人身份、伪造测试或验收结果，以及故意误导用户。

  Plagiarism, impersonation, falsifying test or acceptance results, and deliberately misleading users.

- 利用项目缺陷攻击未经授权的系统，或者在公开讨论中泄露凭据、真实用户数据及尚未协调披露的漏洞细节。

  Exploiting project defects to attack systems without authorization, or exposing credentials, real user data, or vulnerability details before coordinated disclosure in public discussions.

善意提问、不同技术意见和有证据的批评属于正常协作，不能仅因观点不同而受到处罚。

Good-faith questions, differing technical opinions, and evidence-based criticism are normal parts of collaboration. Participants must not be penalized solely for holding different views.

## 报告问题

Reporting Concerns

项目维护者负责处理行为准则报告。维护者入口为仓库所有者 [Ivanluv555](https://github.com/Ivanluv555)；一般协作问题可通过 [项目 Issue](https://github.com/Ivanluv555/agrimart/issues) 反映。

Project maintainers handle code of conduct reports. The repository owner, [Ivanluv555](https://github.com/Ivanluv555), is the maintainer contact point. General collaboration concerns can be raised through [project issues](https://github.com/Ivanluv555/agrimart/issues).

本仓库尚未在文档中公布专用的私密行为举报邮箱。涉及骚扰、隐私或身份信息时，请先发起不含当事人信息和事件细节的联系请求，例如“希望就行为准则问题获取私密联系渠道”，待维护者提供渠道后再提交材料。不要把公开 Issue 当成私密举报渠道。若维护者本人涉及事件，请明确请求由未涉事维护者处理；当前无法保证存在独立的仲裁人员。

This repository has not documented a dedicated email address for private conduct reports. If a concern involves harassment, privacy, or identifying information, first request a private contact channel without including anyone's information or incident details. For example: "I would like a private contact channel for a code of conduct concern." Submit supporting material after a maintainer provides a channel. Public issues are not private reporting channels. If a maintainer is involved in the incident, explicitly request that an uninvolved maintainer handle the report. Independent reviewers are not currently guaranteed to be available.

报告可包含发生时间、交流位置、相关链接、行为描述及希望获得的帮助。只提供处理所需的材料，提交截图或日志前遮盖无关个人信息。安全漏洞请按 [安全政策](SECURITY.md) 报告。

Reports may include when and where the incident occurred, relevant links, a description of the behavior, and the help you are seeking. Provide only the material needed to address the concern, and redact unrelated personal information from screenshots or logs. Report security vulnerabilities according to the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## 处理与申诉

Enforcement and Appeals

维护者应听取相关方说明，按处理所需的最小范围共享报告，并避免披露举报者身份。维护者无法保证平台上的公开材料可被彻底删除。

Maintainers should hear from the parties involved, share reports only as necessary to address them, and avoid disclosing reporters' identities. Maintainers cannot guarantee that public material on the platform can be completely removed.

根据事实、影响和重复发生情况，维护者可以要求修正措辞或删除内容、发出警告、暂时限制参与，或对严重及持续违规者实施长期限制。处理时应说明理由和适用范围；紧急情况下可以先限制有害行为，再补充核实。善意举报不应受到报复。

Depending on the facts, impact, and recurrence, maintainers may request wording changes or content removal, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or impose long-term restrictions for serious or persistent violations. They should explain the reasons and scope of their actions. In urgent situations, they may restrict harmful behavior before completing their review. Good-faith reporting must not lead to retaliation.

当事人可通过原处理渠道提出申诉并补充事实；存在未涉事维护者时，应由其复核。维护者同样受本准则约束。

Those involved may appeal through the original handling channel and provide additional facts. An uninvolved maintainer should review the appeal when one is available. Maintainers are also bound by this code.
